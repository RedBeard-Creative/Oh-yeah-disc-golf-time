Play

How to play

Press SPACE or Launch. The ball shoots up the right-hand lane and enters the table at the top.

Hold A / D or the arrow keys for the flippers. On touchscreens, tap either lower side of the table.

Hit colorful bumpers and all five purple targets to build your multiplier. Three balls per game!

Extra challenge

  • Pink, cyan and yellow bumpers = 100 points.
  • Each purple target = 250 points.
  • Complete all five = 2,000 bonus points.
  • Every 7 bumper hits increases your multiplier.

Nudge helps free a slow or awkwardly positioned ball. You've got 3 per game.
